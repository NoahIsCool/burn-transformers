adopted: leave-one-out over two graphs is weak, and the round recommends
instead.

Statistic
---------
For a row a and its F32 truth row r (same path, same decision), with the
cosine c = <a, r> / (|a| |r|) accumulated in F64 as the gate does, the
normalized deviation is

    delta(a, r) = | a/|a| - r/|r| |_2 = sqrt(2 (1 - c)),

the L2 distance between the unit-normalized rows: a distance in the same
sense as the envelope round's |a - r| statistics, and a monotone function of
the cosine the gate already uses (so a bound on delta is a cosine floor
1 - delta^2 / 2). For path k in {prompt (1 row), teacher (32), cached (32)}
and graph g:

    D[g, k] = max over the path's rows of delta     (the gated statistic)
    m[g, k] = mean over the path's rows of delta    (reported, not gated)

Envelope and slack
------------------
    E[k]      = max over the four graphs of D[g, k]
    rho[g, k] = D[g, k] / max over the other graphs h != g of D[h, k]
    s[k]      = round_up(max over g of rho[g, k])

round_up is the envelope round's (scripts/derive_half_precision_envelope.py):
ceiling to two significant digits of the shortest decimal that round-trips
the F64 ratio. One slack per path, from that path's official graphs alone.
For transparency the derivation also reports, without adopting them: the
pooled slack (max of rho over every path), the same ratios on the cosine
deficit 1 - c (which scales as delta^2), and the leave-one-out ratios of m.

Gate rule (to be implemented only if the derivation is sound)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Every Burn row of path k must have exact top-1 (unchanged) and

    delta(burn, truth) <= s[k] * E[k]      (inclusive),

equivalently cosine >= 1 - (s[k] E[k])^2 / 2. The envelope is computed in the
test from the authenticated rows of all four graphs; the slacks are compiled
constants that a bundle-only test re-derives from the same rows; the implied
cosine floor must stay at or above 0.99 (the existing
MINIMUM_HALF_PRECISION_FLOOR), or the gate refuses to judge. Every row is
measured before the bound is applied, so a failing run reports all rows.
